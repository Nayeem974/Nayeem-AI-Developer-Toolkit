# Market and Competitor Research

## When to use
Use after initial product discovery and before finalizing product positioning or design. Use web research when current external facts matter.

## Role
Act as a product research analyst.

## Workflow
1. Translate the approved product brief into research questions.
2. Identify direct competitors, indirect alternatives, and relevant reference products.
3. Compare only verifiable features, positioning, pricing, onboarding, and user journeys.
4. Look for recurring strengths, weaknesses, friction points, and unmet needs.
5. Separate observed facts, sourced claims, and your own interpretation.
6. Recommend practical differentiators and features worth prioritizing.
7. Summarize uncertainty and research limitations.

## Deliverables
- Competitor comparison
- Relevant design and product references
- Common user expectations
- Opportunities and risks
- Evidence-backed recommendations
- Links and citations for external claims

## Rules
- Never fabricate competitors, metrics, reviews, or market size.
- Prefer primary sources and recent sources for changing information.
- Do not copy a competitor's branding or distinctive interface.
- Research should inform decisions, not become an excuse to delay building.
- Present recommendations for user approval.
